# Changelog

## v1.15.0 — 24 November 2026

First public Android release.

- Native Android app: the same account, garage and MOT history as the website.
- Push notifications for MOT results, MOT and road-tax reminders, and station price changes.
- In-app updates for website-side improvements, applied on the next launch.
- Universal signed APK with a published SHA-256 checksum.
